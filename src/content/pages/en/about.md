LeafLock PDF is a set of tools for everyday PDF work: merging, splitting, rotating, compressing, signing, filling forms, recognizing text in scans and more. Every tool runs inside your web browser, on your own device. Your files are never uploaded to us.

## Why it exists

Most online PDF services work by uploading your file to their servers, processing it there and sending a result back. That means a copy of your contract, tax return, medical letter or ID scan sits, at least for a while, on a computer you don't control, run by a company you may know little about.

For a lot of documents, that's a risk you shouldn't have to take just to merge two files or remove a page. Modern browsers are capable enough to do this work locally, so LeafLock PDF does it locally.

## How it's built

- **Processing happens in your browser.** When you choose a file, it's read by code running on your device. The result is created on your device and saved straight to your downloads. Nothing about the file is sent anywhere.
- **The browser enforces it.** Every page carries a Content-Security-Policy that only allows connections back to leaflockpdf.com, and the site has no code that sends files there. You can check this yourself; [How your files stay private](/security) shows how.
- **Everything comes from our own site.** The supporting files the tools need, such as fonts for rendering pages and the text-recognition model used by [OCR PDF](/ocr-pdf), are served from leaflockpdf.com, not from third-party servers.
- **No account.** You don't need to sign up or sign in to use the tools.
- **No tracking ads.** We don't run advertising or third-party tracking scripts. Showing ads that follow you around the web would contradict the reason the site exists.

## Who it's for

LeafLock PDF is for anyone who works with documents they'd rather not hand to a stranger:

- People handling personal paperwork, such as bank statements, leases, medical records and identity documents.
- Freelancers and small businesses sending contracts, invoices and proposals.
- Professionals with a duty of confidentiality, such as lawyers, accountants and healthcare staff.
- Anyone who simply prefers that their files stay on their own device.

Organizations that need the tools to run entirely inside their own network can use the [Business edition](/business).
