The best way to reach us is by email: [hello@leaflockpdf.com](mailto:hello@leaflockpdf.com).

We read every message. Please allow a few working days for a reply.

## Please don't send us your documents

Never email us confidential documents, and don't attach the file you were working on. We don't need your files to help: LeafLock PDF is built so that your documents stay on your device, and email would undo that.

Instead, describe what happened. These details usually help:

- The tool you were using, for example [Compress PDF](/compress-pdf).
- What you expected, and what happened instead, including the exact wording of any message on the page.
- Your browser and its version, and your device and operating system.
- General facts about the file, if relevant: roughly how many pages it has, its size, whether it's a scan, whether it's password-protected.

If a problem only happens with one particular file, tell us so. We may ask whether you can reproduce it with a file that contains nothing private.

## What to write to us about

### Support

Questions about using a tool, something that doesn't work as expected, or a suggestion for a new feature. Before writing, you might find a quick answer in [Help](/help).

### Business edition

Questions about running LeafLock PDF on your organization's own network, pricing and licensing, or your specific requirements. See [Business edition](/business) for an overview.

### Press

Questions from journalists, writers and researchers about LeafLock PDF or how browser-based processing works. For the technical background, see [How your files stay private](/security).

### Security

If you think you've found a security or privacy problem, please email us with the details. Describe the problem and how to reproduce it; again, there's no need to include any real document.

## What happens to your email

Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation. The [Privacy policy](/privacy) has the details.
