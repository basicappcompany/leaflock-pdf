## Your files

Files you open in a LeafLock PDF tool are processed entirely in your web browser, on your device. This includes the file itself, its name, its contents and the results the tool creates, as well as any camera images used by Scan to PDF and any passwords you type into a tool.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We do not receive, store, view, analyze or log your files or anything about them. The site's Content-Security-Policy instructs your browser to block connections to any server other than our own, and the site has no code that sends files to our server. [How your files stay private](/security) explains how you can check this yourself.

Because we never receive your files, we cannot recover, delete or provide copies of them. They remain under your control on your device.

## Data we process

### Server access logs

When your browser requests a page or file from leaflockpdf.com, our hosting provider, Cloudflare, receives the request and may keep technical logs. These typically contain your IP address, the date and time, the address of the page or file requested, your browser's user-agent string and the response status.

These logs never contain your documents, because documents are not sent in any request.

- **Purpose:** delivering the website, keeping it secure and protecting it from abuse, and diagnosing technical problems.
- **Legal basis (GDPR):** our legitimate interest in running a secure and reliable website (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** [TO CONFIRM: log retention period set by the hosting provider].

### Your account and sign-in history (if you sign in)

Signing in is optional. If you create an account, we keep your email address, when the account was created, your plan, and, if you set them up, a password (stored only as a salted hash) or a link to your Google account. We also keep a record of each time you sign in: the date and time, whether you used an email link, a password or Google, and your browser family and major version (for example "Chrome 141"). We do not store your IP address in this record, and it never contains anything about your files.

If you star tools, we also keep which tools you starred, in the order you starred them, so the same list appears in every browser you sign in on. It is a list of tool names only, such as "Merge PDF", and never contains a file, a file name or anything you typed into a tool.

You can download all of it from your account page, and deleting your account deletes it.

- **Purpose:** running your account, keeping it secure, and showing you when it was used.
- **Legal basis (GDPR):** performing the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in keeping accounts secure (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** until you delete your account. [TO CONFIRM: whether sign-in history should instead expire after a fixed period]

### Feature requests and feedback (if you send one)

The sidebar has two buttons, "Request a feature" and "Send feedback". If you use one, we receive what you type in the box, which of the two it was, and the path of the page you were on (for example "/split-pdf", never a file or anything after the address). If you type an email address into the optional field, we receive that too, and we use it only to reply to you.

We don't store your IP address with your message. It is hashed with a secret key and used only to count how many messages come from one visitor, so a form open to everyone can't be flooded. Your message isn't linked to your account, even if you are signed in, unless you put your email address in it.

Please don't include your documents or anything personal in a message. We never need them.

- **Purpose:** reading what to build or fix next, and replying if you asked us to.
- **Legal basis (GDPR):** your consent, which you give by sending the message (Article 6(1)(a)), and our legitimate interest in improving the product (Article 6(1)(f)).
- **Retention:** until we have dealt with it, and no longer than [TO CONFIRM: retention period, for example 12 months]. To have a message and its email address deleted sooner, email us and say roughly when you sent it and what it said.

Please don't send us your documents. We never need them to help you.

### Error reports

When a tool fails because of a bug, your browser sends a short error report to our own server at leaflockpdf.com, so we can find and fix the problem. No outside error-tracking service is involved.

- **What a report contains:** the type of error and its message, the parts of our own code involved, which tool and page you were using, and your browser's name and main version number (for example “Chrome 141”).
- **What it never contains:** your files, their contents, page text or anything you typed. File names, quoted text and links to local files are removed from the message in your browser before the report is sent.
- **What we don't record:** we don't store your IP address or any cookie with the report.
- **Limits:** at most five reports are sent per page visit, and only for bugs, not for problems such as a password-protected or damaged file.
- **Purpose and legal basis (GDPR):** keeping the tools working, which is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).

## Data stored on your device

- **Theme choice:** if you press the theme button, your light or dark choice is saved in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers it. The default is light, and nothing is saved until you press it.
- **Starred and recent tools:** the tools you star, and the tools you used recently with the time you used them, are saved in your browser's localStorage so the sidebar and your dashboard can show them. Tool names and times only, never a file or a file name. If you are signed in, your starred tools are also saved to your account (see above); the recent list stays on your device. Clear your site data to remove them.
- **OCR language model:** after you first use OCR PDF, your browser stores the English text-recognition model so it doesn't need to download it again. This file is the same for everyone and contains no personal data.
- **Cookies:** two if you sign in (`__Host-leaflock_session`, and `leaflock_signed_in`, which holds only the name of your plan), and a third (`leaflock_skin`) only if you pick a visual skin. None of them track you or carry a profile.

We cannot read any of this data; it stays on your device, except for your starred tools if you are signed in, which are saved to your account as described above. See the [Cookie policy](/cookies) for what each item contains and how to remove it.
