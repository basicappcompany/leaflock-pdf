## Your files

Files you open in a LeafLock PDF tool are processed entirely in your web browser, on your device. This includes the file itself, its name, its contents and the results the tool creates, as well as any camera images used by Scan to PDF and any passwords you type into a tool.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We do not receive, store, view, analyze or log your files or anything about them. The site's Content-Security-Policy instructs your browser to block connections to any server other than our own, and the site has no code that sends files to our server. [How your files stay private](/security) explains how you can check this yourself.

Because we never receive your files, we cannot recover, delete or provide copies of them. They remain under your control on your device.

### Error reports

When a tool fails because of a bug, your browser sends a short error report to our own server at leaflockpdf.com, so we can find and fix the problem. No outside error-tracking service is involved.

- **What a report contains:** the type of error and its message, the parts of our own code involved, which tool and page you were using, and your browser's name and main version number (for example “Chrome 141”).
- **What it never contains:** your files, their contents, page text or anything you typed. File names, quoted text and links to local files are removed from the message in your browser before the report is sent.
- **What we don't record:** we don't store your IP address or any cookie with the report.
- **Limits:** at most five reports are sent per page visit, and only for bugs, not for problems such as a password-protected or damaged file.
- **Purpose and legal basis (GDPR):** keeping the tools working, which is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).

## Data stored on your device

The site doesn't set cookies. It stores the following in your browser, on your device only:

- **Theme choice:** if you choose a light or dark theme, it's saved in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers it. Nothing is stored if you leave it on the system setting.
- **OCR language model:** after you first use OCR PDF, your browser stores the English text-recognition model so it doesn't need to download it again. This file is the same for everyone and contains no personal data.

We cannot read this data; it never leaves your device. See the [Cookie policy](/cookies) for details and how to remove it.
