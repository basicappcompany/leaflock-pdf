Most people who say they want to "edit a PDF" want something modest: add a line of text, put a date in a box, drop in a picture, highlight a clause or correct a typo. PDFs are not word-processor files, so changing the text that is already there is a different and much harder problem than adding new material. This guide explains what you can do, how to do it on your device and where the limits are.

## What editing means here

[Edit PDF](/edit-pdf) adds new content on top of a page. It does not change the text that is already in the file. That is a deliberate boundary: a PDF stores text as positioned pieces, with no flowing paragraphs to reflow.

You can add:

- **Text**, in the size and color you choose
- **Images**, such as a logo
- **Rectangles** and other shapes
- **Highlights**
- **White-out boxes**

Select any item to move, resize, restyle or remove it.

## How to edit a PDF with LeafLock

All editing happens in your browser, so the file stays on your device.

1. Open [Edit PDF](/edit-pdf) and choose your file.
2. Pick a tool and place it on the page.
3. Adjust or remove items by selecting them.
4. Save. You download a new copy; your original is unchanged.

## Replacing a word

You cannot edit existing text directly. To replace a word, cover it with **White-out** and type the new text over it. It works for small corrections: a wrong date, a misspelled name or an old price.

## White-out is not deletion

This matters. White-out covers the text with a white box, but the original is still in the file. It can be copied, found by search or revealed by moving the box. If the text is sensitive, such as an account number you need to remove, white-out is not enough. Use [Redact PDF](/redact-pdf), and read [how to redact a PDF properly](/guides/redact-pdf-properly).

## Fonts and other alphabets

Text is added in a standard sans-serif font, in the size and color you choose. Text in other alphabets, such as Cyrillic or Chinese, is added as an image, so it shows correctly but cannot be selected or searched. Keep that in mind if the result will be processed by other software.

## Undoing a change

Before you save, select any item and remove it. Saving makes a new copy, so your original file stays unchanged. If you realize afterwards something is wrong, go back to the original and redo the edit.

## Good habits

1. Zoom in and check alignment after placing text.
2. Match the size and color of nearby text for small fixes.
3. Keep the original, and name the edited copy clearly.
4. Re-read the whole page after editing, not just the spot you changed.

## Summary

1. Edit PDF adds content over the page; it does not rewrite existing text.
2. White-out hides text but does not remove it.
3. Non-Latin text is added as an image.
4. Your original is never changed.
